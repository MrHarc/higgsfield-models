Three things their pricing page doesn't tell you

1. The "70% cheaper per credit" claim doesn't hold up

Ultra is marketed as having the lowest cost per credit with a "70% cheaper" badge. Against Plus on the same billing period, Ultra annual works out at $0.033 per credit versus Plus annual at $0.039 — about 15% cheaper, not 70%. Ultra is still the better per-credit rate; the number attached to it is not one I can reproduce from their own published prices.

2. Plus is quoted at two different credit amounts

Depending on where you look in Higgsfield's own interface, the Plus plan is described as either 1,000 or 1,200 credits a month. That's a 20% swing on the thing you're buying. The calculator above uses the conservative 1,000. Check what your account actually shows before assuming the higher number.

3. Subscribing through the MCP surface can be cheaper than the website

Higgsfield's checkout links served through its MCP integration showed Plus at $49 monthly and $39 annual, while web pricing for the same tier is commonly listed at $59 and $47. Same plan, different entry point. Compare both before you buy — and note that promotional pricing on this platform changes constantly, so verify rather than trusting either number including mine.

"Just use the API instead" — checking that claim

The standard advice in AI video forums is that Higgsfield is overpriced and you should hit the underlying models directly through an API aggregator, with people quoting figures around 30 cents a video.

Run the numbers and that advice is weaker than it sounds. At roughly 6 credits per video, a 5-second generation costs about 20 cents on Ultra annual if your volume fits inside the plan, 23 cents on Plus annual, and 29 cents at top-up rates. So the subscription route lands at or below the price the API advocates are quoting — before you count the fact that one subscription covers sixteen models, and that an API workflow is something you have to build and maintain.

The API route genuinely wins in one case: sporadic use. If you generate ten videos a month, no subscription makes sense and pay-as-you-go is obviously right. The crossover is volume, not principle — which is what the calculator above is actually measuring.

The honest summary

At low volume, don't subscribe to anything — top-ups are cheaper and plan credits expire unused. Somewhere around 100 videos a month a plan starts winning, and past 500 you're into Ultra plus top-ups. The calculator above finds your crossover point rather than guessing at it. Whatever you pick, cancel auto-renewal on day one: Higgsfield's refund policy covers initial purchases only, not renewals.
