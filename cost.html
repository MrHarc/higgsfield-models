Three things their pricing page doesn't tell you

1. Your video model changes the price nearly fourfold

Nothing in the interface warns you that switching from Kling 3.0 to Seedance 2.0 takes a video from roughly 6 credits to roughly 22.5. On Plus that's the difference between about 200 videos a month and about 53. If your credits vanish faster than expected, this is almost always why.

2. The "70% cheaper per credit" claim doesn't hold up

Ultra carries a "lowest cost per credit — 70% cheaper" badge. Against Plus on the same billing period, Ultra annual is $0.033 per credit versus Plus annual at $0.039 — about 15% cheaper, not 70%. Ultra genuinely is the better rate. The number attached to it isn't one I can reproduce from their own published prices.

3. Subscribing through the MCP surface can be cheaper

Checkout links served through Higgsfield's MCP integration showed Plus at $49 monthly and $39 annual, against $59 and $47 on the website for the same tier. Same plan, different entry point. The MCP offer also quoted 1,000 credits rather than the website's 1,200, so compare the whole package rather than just the headline price.

"Just use the API instead" — checking that claim

The standard forum advice is that Higgsfield is overpriced and you should hit the models directly through an API aggregator, with people quoting around 30 cents a video.

The honest answer is that it depends entirely on which model you'd use, and the gap runs both ways. On Kling 3.0 a subscription video costs roughly 20–29 cents depending on plan — at or below the figure API advocates quote, which makes the "obviously cheaper" claim wrong for that model. On Seedance 2.0 the same video costs roughly 75 cents to a dollar, and there the criticism lands: if Seedance is all you use and you can get it cheaper per-call elsewhere, do that.

The other real case for pay-as-you-go is sporadic use. Ten videos a month justifies no subscription at all, and the calculator above will tell you so.

The honest summary

At low volume, don't subscribe — top-ups are cheaper and plan credits expire unused. A plan starts winning once you're generating steadily, and the crossover depends far more on your video model than on your video count. Whatever you pick, cancel auto-renewal on day one: Higgsfield's refund policy covers initial purchases only, not renewals.
